How BUhostels works

A practical guide to finding a hostel, listing accommodation, and understanding the platform.

Terms of UsePrivacy Policy

For students and visitors

Browse publicly available hostel listings around Busitema University. Search by name or area, then filter by room type, price per semester, gender, distance, and amenities. A listing may show photos, room availability, prices, and owner contact details.

Use the listing's WhatsApp or phone link to contact the owner directly. BUhostels helps people discover listings; it does not take bookings or payments and is not a party to any rental agreement. Confirm availability, inspect the accommodation, and agree on terms with the owner before paying.

For hostel owners

  1. Create an owner account using your name, phone number, email, and password.
  2. Accept the Terms of Use and acknowledge the Privacy Policy to submit registration.
  3. Wait for an administrator to approve your account before publishing listings.
  4. Add hostel details, rooms, semester prices, availability, and photos in your owner dashboard.
  5. Keep your listing accurate and promptly update prices, photos, and availability.

Owner accounts can view listing activity such as views and contact taps. Listings and their photos may be visible to the public while published.

For administrators

Administrators review owner accounts, manage listings, and view site activity. Activity reporting includes visits, hostel views, searches, and WhatsApp or phone contact taps. The platform uses this information to provide dashboards and basic usage analytics.

Platform and data

BUhostels is a web application with a plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript frontend, an Express API, and Supabase for authentication, database, and photo storage. It stores account details, hostel listings, rooms, photos, and activity events. See the Privacy Policy for details about personal information.

Prices are displayed in Ugandan shillings (UGX) per semester. Phone numbers are shown in international format for contact links.
