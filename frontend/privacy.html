Privacy Policy

This policy explains how BUhostels handles information when you browse the site or create an owner account.

Terms of UseHow BUhostels works

Effective and last updated: 17 October 2026.

Information we handle

  • Owner account details: name, email address, phone number, account role and status, and authentication information. Passwords are managed by Supabase Authentication and are not stored in the BUhostels profile table.
  • Hostel listing information: hostel name, description, area and address, contact phone and WhatsApp number, room types, prices, availability, amenities, and photos submitted by owners.
  • Usage events: visits, hostel views, searches, WhatsApp and call link taps, and account or listing actions. A random browser identifier is stored in local storage to estimate unique visitors. Search text may be included in the administrator's activity reports.
  • Technical information: requests sent to the service and information handled by the hosting provider may be processed to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot the site.

How we use information

We use information to create and administer accounts, review owner registrations, publish and manage hostel listings, display contact links, provide owner and administrator dashboards, understand site activity, and protect and maintain the service.

What is public

Published listing details and uploaded listing photos are public. The contact name and phone or WhatsApp details shown on a listing may be visible to anyone. Owner email addresses and account credentials are not displayed on public listings. Do not include sensitive personal information in listing descriptions or photos.

Storage and service providers

BUhostels uses Supabase for authentication, database, and photo storage. Depending on how the project is deployed, the application and frontend may also be hosted by providers such as Render or Cloudflare. These providers process information as needed to operate their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

When you choose to call or message a hostel owner, you leave BUhostels for your phone service or WhatsApp. Those services are independent and handle information under their own policies.

Browser storage and analytics

The site uses browser local storage to keep an owner's sign-in session and a random visitor identifier, and session storage to avoid counting repeated visits within a browsing session. These identifiers are used for the site features and aggregate visitor reporting described above. You can clear browser storage in your browser settings; clearing a sign-in session signs you out.

Retention and account requests

Account and listing information is kept while an account or listing is active and for as long as needed to operate and protect the service. Activity records do not currently have an automatic expiry period and may remain in reporting records until the site administrator removes them. Deleting an owner account removes its profile and associated hostel records, but may not remove already-created activity records.

To request access, correction, or deletion of account information, contact the BUhostels site administrator through the support contact provided for this service. Some information may need to be retained for operational or legal reasons.

Security and children

We use access controls and server-side authentication to protect account and administrative features. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. BUhostels is intended for people seeking or listing accommodation, not for children to create accounts independently.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published. If a material change affects owner accounts, we may ask owners to review and accept the updated terms and policy before continuing to use account features.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the BUhostels site administrator using the support contact provided for this service.

This privacy information describes the current project features and is not legal advice.

BUhostels helps Busitema University students find accommodation and contact hostel owners directly.
