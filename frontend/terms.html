1. About BUhostels

BUhostels is a listing and discovery service intended to help Busitema University students find hostels and contact listing owners directly. It is not a landlord, letting agent, booking service, payment processor, or party to any accommodation agreement.

Registration alone does not authorize you to publish listings; an administrator must approve your account. You are responsible for activity carried out through your account and should promptly report suspected unauthorized access to the site administrator.

3. Owner listing responsibilities

If you publish a listing, you confirm that you are authorized to provide the information and photos and that the listing is accurate, current, and not misleading. You are responsible for updating prices, availability, contact details, and other listing content. Do not upload content that you do not have permission to use, that violates another person's rights, or that is unlawful, abusive, or deceptive.

Published listing details, contact information, and uploaded photos may be publicly visible. Only include information you are willing to share publicly.

4. Browsing and contacting owners

Visitors may use listing information to make enquiries. Visitors and owners are responsible for independently verifying accommodation details, availability, identity, prices, payment requests, and any rental agreement. Inspect the accommodation and agree on terms directly with the owner before making a payment.

BUhostels does not guarantee listing accuracy, availability, suitability, owner identity, accommodation quality, or the outcome of any contact or transaction. No booking or payment is made through BUhostels. To the extent allowed by law, the platform is provided on an “as available” basis.
